How a take works

01

Pick the scene

Eight scenarios — quit your job, break up, come out, ask for a raise, confront a parent, say no to family, win them back, custom.

02

Set up the other person

Their name. Your relationship to them. Pick three reaction traits — cold, defensive, cries, silent, loud, manipulative, warm, rational, bargaining.

03

Run the scene

Type or talk. They reply in character. Algorithmic, on-device — no cloud, no recording.

04

The Watcher reports

How many times you said "sorry". Where you softened your own line. One concrete suggestion for take 2.

What it isn't

  • It is not a therapist. It will not tell you what to do.
  • It does not auto-edit your words to be "softer".
  • It does not store your conversations in the cloud.
  • It has no leaderboard, share button, or streak.
  • It will not sell you a BetterHelp affiliate link.

Privacy in one line

Your dialogue never leaves your phone. The library only saves metadata and your final reflection — encrypted at rest with AES-GCM. Voice transcription is on-device.
